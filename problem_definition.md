# Problem Definition

In many universities, event management is still handled manually using paper-based systems or scattered digital tools. This leads to problems such as data redundancy, miscommunication, lack of proper scheduling, and difficulty in tracking participant information.

Students often miss important events due to poor communication, and organizers face challenges in managing registrations and approvals. There is no centralized system to manage all events efficiently. Therefore, a reliable and automated University Event and Management System is required to solve these issues.
